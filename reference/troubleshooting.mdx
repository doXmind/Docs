title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Resolve common Page, Attachment, and legacy recovery issues."
---

## A file does not appear in the workspace

- Confirm that it is a Markdown Page or supported local Attachment.
- Refresh or reopen the folder after changing files outside doXmind.
- PDF, spreadsheet, and HTML files are Attachments; DOCX, PPTX, standalone images, and arbitrary other extensions are not promised as workspace documents.

## An Attachment is read-only

That is expected. PDF, spreadsheet, and HTML files are not Pages. Use **Open Externally** to work in the system application or **Reveal** to find the source file.

Opening an ordinary Attachment does not create a sidecar or rewrite the source.

## Legacy recovery is unavailable or incomplete

An older Page, PDF, or spreadsheet sidecar can be corrupt, unsupported, or contain state that the isolated recovery bridge cannot interpret. doXmind reports that conservatively instead of treating evidence as empty.

Keep the original source, main sidecar, every `.bak`, `.lock`, and `.corrupt-*` artifact together. Do not rename, combine, replace, migrate, or delete them. Exporting a recovery report preserves those files and records the exact legacy bytes; it does not recreate the retired editor.

## A Markdown Page changed outside doXmind

The external `.md` or `.markdown` source remains authoritative. Resolve the visible conflict before making more edits. A normal Page save does not refresh or create a same-name sidecar.

## Hidden legacy artifacts are not visible

In Finder or the macOS Trash, press `Cmd+Shift+.` to show hidden files. A sidecar begins with a dot and ends in `.doxmind`; related evidence can include `.bak`, `.lock`, and `.corrupt-*` suffixes.

- Your doXmind version from **Settings → About**

- The exact message shown in the app

Do not attach private documents unless you intend to share their contents with support.
