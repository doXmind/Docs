Quick Edit is one of doXmind's most powerful features. Select any text and instantly transform it with AI assistance - no need to open the chat panel.

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select text">
    Highlight the text you want to edit in your document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Quick Edit menu">
    Right-click to open the context menu, or use the floating toolbar that appears above your selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a command">
    Select from the available AI commands like "Improve Writing" or "Translate".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or dismiss">
    Review the AI-generated result. Click to accept the changes, or press Escape to keep the original text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Commands

### Writing Improvements

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Fix Grammar** | Correct spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors |
| **Improve Writing** | Enhance clarity, flow, and overall quality |
| **Simplify** | Make the text easier to understand |
| **Make Longer** | Expand the content with more detail |
| **Make Shorter** | Condense the text while preserving meaning |

## Ask in Chat

At the bottom of the Quick Edit menu, you'll find "Ask in Chat". This option sends your selected text to the AI Chat panel where you can:

- Ask specific questions about the text
- Request custom transformations
- Get detailed explanations or feedback

<Tip>
Quick Edit is perfect for fast, targeted improvements. For more complex edits or when you need to explain what you want, use "Ask in Chat" to have a conversation with the AI.
</Tip>

## Real-Time Preview

When you select a Quick Edit command, the AI processes your text and shows the result in real-time. You can see the changes before accepting them, making it easy to decide if the edit is what you wanted.
