The Knowledge Base feature lets you upload documents and have the AI search, read, and reference them when answering your questions. This is powered by RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) - semantic vector search that finds relevant information in your files.

## What is Knowledge Base?

When you upload documents to your conversation, doXmind:

1. Extracts the text content from your files
2. Splits the content into searchable chunks
3. Creates semantic embeddings for intelligent search
4. Enables the AI to find and cite relevant passages

This means you can ask questions about your documents and get accurate answers with references to the source material.

## How to Upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat panel">
    Click the chat icon to open the AI assistant panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your files">
    Drag and drop files directly into the chat area, or click the attachment button to browse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    The file is processed and indexed. You'll see a confirmation when it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions">
    Start chatting! The AI will automatically search your uploaded documents when relevant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How RAG Search Works

When you ask a question, the AI:

1. **Searches** your uploaded documents for relevant passages
2. **Retrieves** the most relevant chunks of text
3. **Generates** an answer using both the retrieved information and its own knowledge
4. **Cites** the source documents when appropriate

This ensures answers are grounded in your actual documents rather than just general AI knowledge.
