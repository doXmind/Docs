doXmind supports image analysis powered by Claude Vision. Upload images in your chat and let the AI describe, analyze, and extract information from them.

## What AI Can Do With Images

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Describe Images" icon="image">
    Get detailed descriptions of photos, illustrations, or any visual content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extract Text (OCR)" icon="text">
    Pull text from screenshots, photos of documents, or images with text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understand Charts" icon="chart-line">
    Analyze charts, graphs, and diagrams. Get insights about the data they represent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design Feedback" icon="palette">
    Share mockups or designs and get feedback on layout, typography, and visual elements.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Upload Images

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste from clipboard">
    Copy an image and paste it directly into the chat input with `Ctrl+V` (or `Cmd+V` on Mac).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag and drop">
    Drag image files from your computer into the chat panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the upload button">
    Click the image upload button in the chat input area to browse for files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Formats

- PNG
- JPEG / JPG
- GIF
- WebP

**Limit:** Up to 10 images per message, maximum 5MB per image.

## Example Use Cases

### Screenshot Analysis
Upload a screenshot and ask:
- "What does this error message mean?"
- "Summarize the information shown here"
- "Extract the text from this image"

### Document Photos
Take a photo of a printed document and ask:
- "Transcribe this handwritten note"
- "What are the key points in this document?"

### Data Visualization
Share charts or graphs and ask:
- "What trends does this chart show?"
- "Summarize the data in this graph"
- "What's the highest value shown?"

### Design Review
Upload mockups or designs and ask:
- "How could this layout be improved?"
- "Is the text readable against this background?"
- "What's the visual hierarchy here?"

<Tip>
Combine image analysis with your document context. For example, upload a chart related to your document and ask the AI to help you write a description of it.
</Tip>
