---

AI Autocomplete provides intelligent text suggestions as you write, similar to GitHub Copilot for code. It helps you write faster by predicting what comes next based on your context.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start writing">
    As you type, the AI analyzes your document context and writing style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See suggestions">
    Suggestions appear as faded gray text after your cursor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept with Tab">
    Press `Tab` to accept the suggestion and insert it into your document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep typing to dismiss">
    If you don't want the suggestion, simply continue typing. The suggestion will disappear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Context-Aware Suggestions

The AI considers:

- **Your document content** - Suggestions match the topic and style of what you're writing
- **Recent text** - The AI pays attention to your most recent sentences for continuity
- **Document type** - Technical documents get technical suggestions, creative writing gets creative suggestions

## Tips for Better Suggestions

<Tip>
**Write clear sentences** - The AI gives better suggestions when it understands your intent from the preceding text.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Use proper formatting** - Headings and structure help the AI understand the context of each section.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Be patient at paragraph starts** - Suggestions work best mid-sentence when there's more context to work with.
</Tip>

## When to Use Autocomplete

Autocomplete is most helpful when:

- Writing repetitive content
- Completing common phrases
- Continuing a thought you've started
- Drafting content quickly

<Note>
Autocomplete suggestions are generated in real-time and adapt to your writing style. The more you write, the better the suggestions become.
</Note>
