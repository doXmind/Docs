## What is doXmind?

doXmind is an AI-powered writing assistant that helps you write better, faster. Think of it as **"Cursor for Writing"** - bringing the power of AI assistance to document creation.

Powered by Claude AI with 200K token context, doXmind understands your entire document and provides intelligent assistance throughout your writing process.
