The doXmind interface is designed for focused writing with AI assistance always within reach. Here's how the workspace is organized.

## Desktop Layout

The desktop interface is organized into three main areas:

## Header Controls

The header bar at the top provides quick access to:

| Control | Function |
|---------|----------|
| **Chat toggle** | Open/close the AI chat panel |
| **Theme toggle** | Switch between light and dark mode |
| **Save status** | Shows when your document was last saved |

## Sidebar

The left sidebar is your file management hub:

- **New document** - Click the + button to create a new file
- **File list** - All your documents in one place
- **Search** - Find files by name or content
- **Import** - Bring in PDF, Word, or Markdown files

Click a file to open it in the editor. Right-click for additional options like rename or delete.

## Editor Area

The center of the screen is your writing space:

- **Toolbar** - Quick access to formatting options
- **Writing area** - Where you create your content
- **Floating menus** - Context-sensitive options appear when you select text

## AI Chat Panel

The right panel (when open) contains:

- **Chat messages** - Your conversation with the AI
- **Input area** - Type messages or drag files
- **Context indicators** - Shows what the AI knows about your document

<Tip>
You can resize panels by dragging their edges. Collapse the sidebar for a distraction-free writing experience.
</Tip>

## Theme Toggle

Switch between light and dark mode using the theme toggle in the header. Your preference is saved automatically and persists across sessions.
